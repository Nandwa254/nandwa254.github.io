Operations CRM & Workflow Platform

An operations CRM and workflow platform built around customer records, work queues, follow-ups, ownership and reporting.

Product & systemsHTML · CSS · JavaScriptGoogle Apps Script · Sheets · PHP

The problem

Make fragmented operational work easier to manage.

Customer records, follow-ups, assignments and reporting can become fragmented across manual workflows. The result is slower reporting and less visibility into ownership and status.

What was built

One operating layer for records, actions and visibility.

The platform connects users, records, workflow and automation with a structured data layer and reporting / monitoring.

My role

Workflow design through implementation.

I led the workflow and information-structure design, translated operating requirements, shaped the interface, implemented the system and worked on automation and reporting.

Design considerations

Keep the workflow simple while making control visible.

The main design principles were workflow before screens, visibility at the point of action, automation around repetitive work, reporting around decisions, and controls and exceptions inside the workflow.
