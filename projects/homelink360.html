HomeLink360

A founder-built, housing-led financial-services venture designed around the real cash-flow and trust problems people face when securing or moving into urban homes.

FounderProduct & operationsKenya2025 — present

The problem

The problem

Urban renters can face a combination of upfront rent/deposit costs, moving expenses, uncertain property information and limited access to short-term support. HomeLink360 was conceived around the actual move-and-settle journey rather than around a generic lending proposition.

The product thesis

The product thesis

The operating model begins with a housing need, provides housing search/placement and move-related services, then uses verified customer information and repayment behaviour to support appropriate next-stage financial products. This makes the relationship broader than a single transaction.

My role

My role

I am responsible for the venture’s product and operating design: customer journeys, product workflows, digital processes, pilot learning, operational controls and the evolution of the business model.
