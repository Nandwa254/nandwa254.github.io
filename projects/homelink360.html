HomeLink360

A housing-led financial-services venture built around the cash-flow and trust challenges people face when securing or moving into urban homes.

FounderProduct & operationsKenya2025 — present

The problem

Start with the move-and-settle journey.

Upfront rent and deposit costs, moving expenses, uncertain property information and limited access to short-term support can make securing a home difficult.

The product starts with that housing need rather than with a generic lending proposition.

The product model

Housing first, then appropriate financial support.

The operating model begins with housing search and placement and move-related services. Verified customer information and repayment behaviour can then support appropriate next-stage financial products.

The aim is to build a useful relationship around a real customer need rather than treat the financial product as the starting point.

My role

Founder responsibility across product and operations.

I lead the venture's product and operating design, including customer journeys, product workflows, digital processes, pilot learning, operating controls and the evolution of the business model.

Evidence

Early operating evidence.

The same operating work shows repeat-customer behaviour and movement between housing services, housing-linked support and later products. This is early-stage evidence, not mature portfolio performance.
