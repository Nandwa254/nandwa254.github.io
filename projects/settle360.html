The operating proposition

Connect client requirements to controlled execution.

Many assignments depend on work happening consistently beyond the office. Teams must be organised and deployed, responsibilities made clear, field activity verified, exceptions resolved and results brought back into a usable reporting loop.

Settle360 is built around that delivery requirement. The emphasis is not simply supplying people or completing visits. It is establishing an operating structure that connects the assignment brief to field execution, verification and close-out.

My role

Venture leadership and end-to-end operating responsibility.

As Founder & Operations Lead, I shape the service proposition and operating model and lead delivery routines across client assignments. This includes translating client requirements into delivery plans, organising team recruitment and deployment, setting responsibilities and targets, directing verification and quality checks, maintaining reporting and reconciliation, and resolving delivery bottlenecks.
