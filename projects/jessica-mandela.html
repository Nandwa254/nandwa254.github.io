Jessica Mandela — Artist Website

A client website built for gospel artist Jessica Mandela to bring music, podcasts, videos, biography, gallery content and professional enquiries into one place.

Client projectWordPressResponsive webCompleted

The brief

Give the artist a clear public home for her work.

The site needed to strengthen the artist's online presence while making it easy for visitors to discover music, watch content, learn about her work and make professional enquiries.

What was built

One responsive website across content, media and enquiries.

The site brings together an artist-focused homepage, music and listening-platform links, podcasts, video, biography, gallery content and a contact / booking workflow.

The experience was designed to work across desktop and mobile while keeping the visual identity consistent throughout the site.

My role

From WordPress setup to deployment.

I handled the website from setup through delivery, including UX and page structure, visual design, Elementor implementation, responsive layout, media integrations, contact and booking workflow, SEO configuration, SMTP and email setup, hosting configuration, testing and troubleshooting.
