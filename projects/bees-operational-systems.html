Operational Systems & Automation

Three internal systems I designed while working across operations and product responsibilities at Bees Credit / Choice Bank. Each started with a practical operating problem and was built to make the work more visible, measurable and easier to manage.

Bees Credit / Choice BankOperations · Product · SystemsInternal tools

The common problem

I kept finding gaps between the process and the information needed to manage it.

People were doing the work, but it was not always easy to see where time was being lost, who owned an issue, or where a loan stood in the process.

I did not treat each problem as a separate reporting exercise. I designed practical tools around the actual workflow.

01 · Loan process time calculator

Make every minute in the loan process visible.

I designed an Excel-based time-calculation system that captured how long a loan file spent at each process stage and with each person it passed through.

I used the system to compare actual time against expected time and identify where a file was slowing down. We were working toward disbursement targets of 6 hours for logbook loans and 12 hours for asset finance loans, measured from loan origination to disbursement.

This changed the conversation from “the process is slow” to “this is the stage and handoff where time is being lost.” The wider workflow redesign and automation work resulted in a 70% reduction in process turnaround time.

02 · Branch issue escalation & resolution system

Bring branch issues into one operating system.

I also designed a unified escalation framework for branch operational issues. Before that, different issues could be raised and followed up through different channels, making ownership and escalation inconsistent.

I mapped the issue categories, ownership, escalation levels, expected response and resolution flow, then designed the supporting Apps Script-based system around that framework.

I wanted a branch to know where to raise an issue, who owns it, what should happen next and when it needs to move up the chain.

I designed the framework to bring different branches and different issue types into one consistent operating model.

03 · Imarika loan tracking system

Give operations and finance one view of the loan portfolio.

While helping build the Imarika MSME lending product, I designed a custom loan-tracking system used by operations and finance to follow loans through the process and monitor the information needed for delivery and repayment support.

I designed the system around operational visibility rather than simply storing records. I used it to connect the product workflow to the day-to-day work of the teams responsible for moving and monitoring the loans.

I did this alongside my field research and product responsibilities. I mapped 400+ businesses across three counties and used what I learned from the field to help shape customer journeys, operating processes and supporting systems.

My role

I was the person connecting the operating problem to the system.

My role was not simply to request a tool from technology. I worked through the operating problem, designed the workflow and logic, built or shaped the supporting system, and worked with the teams who had to use it.

That is the part of systems work I enjoy most: understanding what the business is trying to achieve, then turning it into something people can actually use.
