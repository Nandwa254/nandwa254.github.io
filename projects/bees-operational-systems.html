Operational Systems & Automation

Three internal systems designed around practical operating problems at Bees Credit / Choice Bank: loan-stage time measurement, branch issue escalation and Imarika loan tracking.

Bees Credit / Choice BankOperations · Product · SystemsPrivate implementation

Context

Turn operational problems into measurable workflows.

The systems addressed three recurring gaps: limited visibility into where loan-processing time was being spent, inconsistent ownership of branch issues, and fragmented information around loan tracking.

01 · Loan process time calculator

Make the time in the loan process visible.

An Excel-based calculation system measured the time a loan file spent at each process stage and with each person handling it.

The work was aligned to targets of 6 hours for logbook loans and 12 hours for asset finance loans, measured from loan origination to disbursement.

Result: 70% reduction in process turnaround time through the wider workflow redesign and automation work.

02 · Branch issue escalation & resolution

Give branch issues a consistent path.

A unified framework was designed around issue categories, ownership, escalation levels, response expectations and resolution flow.

I designed the supporting Apps Script-based system around that framework so branches could see where to raise an issue, who owns it, what happens next and when it needs to be escalated.

03 · Imarika loan tracking

Connect the product workflow to operations and finance.

A custom loan-tracking platform supported operations and finance in following and monitoring loans through the process.

I designed the tracking around the actual workflow and used field and product learning to shape what the teams needed to see.

400+ businesses mapped across three counties during the wider Imarika product research and pilot work.
