Client Portal Application

A mobile-first customer portal built around onboarding, service access, document flow, messaging and status visibility.

Product & workflow designMobile-firstHTML · CSS · JavaScriptApps Script · Sheets · PHP

The problem

Bring scattered customer interactions into one clearer flow.

When onboarding, documents, support conversations and service updates are spread across different channels, it becomes harder for customers to see what is required and what happens next.

What was built

A mobile-first customer interaction layer.

The portal brings together guided onboarding, secure access, service requests, document and image submission, messaging, status visibility and the internal workflow supporting those interactions.

My role

Product thinking through implementation.

I led product thinking, information architecture, customer-journey design, workflow structure, interface direction, implementation and the access-control approach.

Product model

01customer
02guided onboarding
03secure access
04service request
05supporting information
06operations workflow
07status / communication

Design considerations

Reduce friction without losing operational visibility.

The design priorities were clear next actions, fewer repeated information requests, stronger links between customer activity and internal work, and a mobile experience built for real use rather than a desktop screen compressed onto a phone.

Status

The production implementation is associated with a live business environment, so source code, customer information and proprietary configuration remain private.
