How I work

Turn the business problem into an operating system.

I tend to start with the real workflow: who does what, what can go wrong, what information is missing, where decisions stall, and what needs to be visible. From there, I work backwards into process design, controls, metrics, tooling and team routines.

01Understand the frontline reality before designing the solution.
02Make ownership, handoffs and exceptions visible.
03Use data to decide what needs fixing and whether the fix lasts.
04Keep systems practical enough for teams to actually use.
